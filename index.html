Net worth breakdown

    How it is calculated

    Stocks: the cost is for your next benefit block. Each new block needs twice as many shares as the previous one, so the return drops the more blocks you own. Shares can be sold back (0.1 % fee), but the price can move. Benefit values come from the item market and points market; energy is valued against Xanax (250 energy).
    City Bank: Torn's base rates plus your Bank Interest merits (+5 % per level). Only one investment at a time, max $2,000,000,000. TCI adds +10 % on top, valued as the extra interest on your running investment, or on a full $2b if you have none.
    Optimal allocation = the same total capital (stocks with known benefit values, TCI, any bank deposit and spare cash) placed in the benefit blocks and bank options that pay the most per dollar. Other passive benefits, benefits without a value and a running bank deposit count as locked and are left alone. Price movements are not included.
    Items: valued at Torn's market price per item. Torn caches the inventory for up to an hour, so recent changes can take a while to show.
